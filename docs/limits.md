# What a demand test cannot tell

- **Whether the visitors were the right people.** A rate from visitors outside the target audience answers a different question. Count only visitors from the channels aimed at that audience.
- **Whether a sign-up means a sale.** An email is a small commitment; a chosen plan is more; a deposit is the most. Read the verdict in the light of what was asked.
- **Changes during the test.** If the page, the price or the channels changed half way, the visitors before and after saw different tests. Split them into variants.
- **Peeking.** Checking the result every day and stopping the moment it looks good makes a false pass more likely. Decide in advance how many visitors the test gets, or use the 99 percent level when you look often.
- **Many variants.** With many variants, one may look ahead by chance. Treat a lead found among many as a hint to test again, not a result.
